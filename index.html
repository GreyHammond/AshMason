WELCOME TO ASHMASON

A world with somewhere to go.

Start in town, earn your keep, build your reputation, and decide how far you want to take the adventure. The world rewards players who fight, explore, gather, trade, and keep pushing forward.

⚔

Bodyguards

Recruit a companion who follows you, grows through kills, takes better gear, and enters dungeons beside you.

✦

Quests

Take jobs from the town, earn coins and Quest Points, and unlock new opportunities as your reputation grows.

◇

Kits

Pim helps new adventurers get established with a compass and free gear when they arrive.

◈

Fluctuating Economy

Market prices shift through the day. Watch for HOT items and decide when to buy or sell.

dDUNGEONS — REBORN

Every descent is different.

The Gatekeeper sends adventurers into procedurally generated dungeons. Three difficulty tiers demand more Quest Points and coins. Each run gives you an objective: clear the spawners, defeat a boss, or recover a lost item.

YOUR COMPANION

More than a bodyguard.

Recruit a fighter through the Barracks Captain. Your bodyguard levels from kills and can be outfitted with better gear as they grow. When they hold position, you can deploy a camp: a portable retreat with storage and utility stations.
