Not a hub. Not PvP. Not another vanilla box.

AshMason is a built world with a town, a market, a guild of hunters, exiles with stories, and a procedural dungeon at the edge of the map. You show up, earn your keep, and slowly become someone the town knows.

The people you'll meet

Each one holds a door. Some open the first day. Some wait until the town decides you're worth talking to.

  1. Pim Hands out the town compass and a couple of free kits when you arrive. Come back every four hours. Open
  2. Quartermaster Posts starter jobs — clear a threat, gather a stack of something, earn your first Quest Points. Open
  3. Odell Grocer. Drops crops and materials into his crate; he pays for what he wants and hands the rest back. Open
  4. Midge, Wren, Gus The three open stalls. Food, arrows, tools. Prices move every twenty minutes. Open
  5. Barracks Captain Sells you a bodyguard for 100 coins. Warrior or Ranger. Fittings, camp gear, and a class you can grow through ten tiers. 100 coins
  6. Flint Monster hunter. Harder contracts, better coin — pillagers, ravagers, and worse. 15 QP
  7. Ash An exile with a nether-warped past. Help him through his chain and Thistle's apothecary opens for you. 15 QP
  8. Vale Ender-touched. Won't speak to strangers. Finish her chain and Nook's stall opens — and further doors after that. 25 QP
  9. Thistle Apothecary. Potions, brewing materials, nether goods. Ash chain
  10. Nook Curiosities from the End. Later on, the road to elytra runs through her too. Vale chain
  11. Gatekeeper Takes your key, opens the doors, drops you into a dungeon at your tier. Key
  12. Xumar Posts daily bounties near Flint. Three contracts, rotated every day. Bounty pay covers your dungeon entry and stacks on top of clear rewards. Daily

dDungeons Reborn

Every descent generates itself.

The Gatekeeper takes your key and drops you into a dungeon that didn't exist five seconds ago. Layouts, mobs, spawner banks, loot — all rolled at entry. Every run gives you an objective: clear the spawners, kill the boss, or recover a marked item. Complete it and you're paid on the spot. Die or walk out early and you leave with what you can carry.

Bring up to three friends. /party create, share the code, dive in together. Bodyguards come too.

The Long Road

What all of this is leading to.

Every quest, every bounty, every clean dungeon run pushes you further into a story the town has been telling itself for a while. The Quartermaster teaches you to hold your own. Flint teaches you to hunt. Ash teaches you what it's like to be exiled from a place you can't stop thinking about.

And Vale knows things about flight.

About wings. About what it takes to leave the ground for good. But she won't share any of it with someone the town doesn't trust. Prove yourself — through combat, through exploration, through the things you can only find in open air, deep underground, or at the bottom of the ocean — and eventually she'll show you the way up.
